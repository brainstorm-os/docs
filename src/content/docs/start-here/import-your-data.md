---
title: Import your data
description: Bring your existing knowledge into Brainstorm — from Notion, Anytype, CSV, Markdown, or plain files on disk.
---

Brainstorm is only useful with your knowledge in it. There are importers for the common sources, and because everything lands as typed [objects](/concepts/objects/), imported content is immediately linkable, searchable, and automatable — not a read-only archive.

## From Notion

Connect your Notion account and pull pages straight across — **no export file to download first**. Pages arrive as clean notes; you can also import a Markdown export if you'd rather not connect the account.

## From Anytype

The Anytype importer brings your objects across with their files — pasted screenshots included. Tasks and bookmarks become real [Tasks](/apps/tasks/) and [Bookmarks](/apps/bookmarks/) rather than plain notes, and imported notes keep their titles. Imports run in the background with progress and cancel.

## From CSV

[Database](/apps/database/) imports CSV with type inference — point it at a spreadsheet export and you get a typed List with real properties, ready for views, filters, and formulas.

## Files and media

Drag files in from your file manager, or upload through the OS picker — bytes, MIME type, size, and hash included. Every file becomes a vault object you can link, tag, and preview. See [Files](/apps/files/).

## From the web

The built-in [Browser](/apps/browser/) clips any page into a Bookmark with its readable content attached, and files you download in it save straight into your vault.
