**Agent.** A chat surface over the shared AI broker: run a local Ollama model or bring your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or GLM. The agent builds real things with you — notes, tasks, events, whole databases, even apps — proposed as cards you approve before anything is saved. Conversations are vault objects, answers cite the objects they drew from, and the agent can only act within capabilities you granted.

## What it can do

- **Propose & approve** — ask for a note, task, event, bookmark, or contact and the agent drafts it as a card in chat. Edit a field, approve what's right, discard the rest. Nothing is written to your vault until you approve it — even a hidden instruction in a document can only draft a card, never save one.
- Propose a database row — or design a whole new database — the same way: as a card you review before anything lands.
- Draft a reply as an email and hand it straight to the [Mailbox](/apps/mailbox) composer, ready to send.
- Ask it for an app and it drafts the manifest and code as files you read and approve, then [install from your vault](/apps/code-editor).
- Hybrid retrieval grounds every answer in your vault, with citations that open the source object.
- Attach context explicitly: @-mention documents and people, drop in text files, or add images for vision models.
- Long-term memory is opt-in and off by default — every remembered item is visible, redactable, and deletable.
- "Summarize with the agent" and "Ask the agent about this" appear on objects across the other apps.
- Save a conversation as an automation: its tool trace becomes a workflow, opened in the builder for review.
- Fail-closed capability model: the agent's tools are always a subset of what you granted the conversation, re-checked on every call.
